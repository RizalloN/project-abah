# Expressive financial studio

The user's new direction is a much stronger luxury 3D presentation. This pass
keeps the established financial reading plane, while increasing the amplitude,
staging and visible material depth of its graphic objects. BRI blue and navy
light clear glass, reflected metal and the existing source-specific colors.
The KC polygon zoom and lateral detail/back handoff remain the geographic spine.

## The visible score

- A section enters through a broad but local light curtain. Its heading and
  source figures remain available while a thin luminous edge establishes the
  next reading boundary.
- Glass vessels assemble in depth and fill with their actual source fractions.
  Sharper glass edges, reflected shoulders, liquid surface depth and stronger
  contact shadows make capacity and customer share tangible.
- Sector prisms assemble as separate faces, with distinct light on the front,
  top and side. Their final heights, labels and source ratios remain unchanged.
- Opportunity spheres receive pearl-like material and a pronounced contact
  shadow. The sphere's recorded center, clickable target and axes do not drift.
- Source vectors and time curves draw through a lit field; donut faces and
  walls assemble within their reserved stage. Outside legends stay readable.
- Exact tables stay aligned while the boundary light sweeps. Metrics receive
  localized accents. Controls respond to press and keyboard focus rather than
  orbiting or tilting under the pointer.

## CSS/runtime boundary

The deck adds `.sg-expressive-motion`. Actual `data-sg-motion` component roots
receive `.is-expressive-entering` while their owned Web Animations sequence is
active. The runtime supplies `--expressive-pass` (0..1 visible passage) and
`--expressive-impulse` (0..1 entry energy). The runtime owns first-entry staging,
animation handles, cancellation, view replacement and reduced-motion bypass.
CSS supplies the physical surfaces and local supporting planes; it does not
change financial values, source ratios or geographic identities.

Eligible roots receive one inert `span.sg-expressive-plane` containing
`.sg-expressive-plane__floor` and `.sg-expressive-plane__light`. That span is an
absolute clipped frame behind content, so it contributes no layout height and
does not mask or capture financial controls. Animate its inner floor/light to
keep the outer bounds reserved. Metrics receive one plane inside each existing
panel `div`, without inserting spans directly into the description list.

Each actual DOM object owns a timed assembly even when a large wheel jump has
already completed its reading entry. The stagger is capped at 300ms; material
resolves in roughly 1-2 seconds and restores the exact settled CSS projection
before releasing its handles. Explicit chart/source interactions may replace
the old owner and replay material. Hidden views, detached nodes, focus, reduced
motion and disposal cancel their work while retaining complete source geometry.

Donut disc grouping containers use `filter: none` and `preserve-3d`; shadows live
on the outer stage so layer depth survives. The grouping constraint follows
[CSS Transforms 2](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-transforms-2/#grouping-property-values).

Each component has its own stacking context; the geographic chapter does not
gain an opaque stacking context above or below the shared world by this layer.
The map's actual reserved rectangle and canvas projection remain stationary
through decorative choreography. No mouse-hover orbit, card tilt, modal, native
select, generated video, new dependency or modified data formula is introduced.

The original entry latch still keeps source geometry complete after the one-shot
sequence. Subsequent scroll affects decorative light and depth; it must not
empty previously read data. Phone sequences use the same figures with shorter
depth travel. Reduced motion keeps complete figures and physical material while
skipping the assembly and moving light.

## Evidence before completion

Capture several distinct frames during actual 1000–1500 ms first-entry
assemblies and a settled frame. Compare them to establish visible movement,
rather than inferring animation quality from a final screenshot. Recheck stable
row/axis/label geometry, exact text, chart bounds, reverse scroll, focus, touch,
reduced motion and interrupted lateral transitions. This document describes the
design contract and does not claim those runtime checks have already passed.
