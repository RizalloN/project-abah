# Sector intelligence and live scroll, 26 October 2026

An iteration of the existing working atlas, not a replacement website. The user
requested the supplied business-population Google Sheet, September SSA CRAS,
clearer bubble axes, removal of the repeated population slab, and richer scroll
motion that remains professional. Existing branch-first polygon selection,
KC overlays, keyboard/touch use, and the three-point reading rail remain.

User-confirmed formulas:

- Potential is column F, summed across unique mapped service districts for a
  UKER. UKER territories can overlap. KC/Area totals use original unique geography.
- Displayed penetration is normalized to 0–100%; counts and raw ratios remain.
- A fine CRAS subsector uses the parent category's UKER service-area population
  as its denominator. This is named parent-reference coverage, not an invented
  exclusive fine-subsector population. A zero-row finer filter keeps its fixed
  selected territory.
