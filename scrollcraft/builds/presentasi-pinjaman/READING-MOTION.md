# Motion score for financial reading

The presentation retains the selected KC, real polygon zoom, lateral map/detail
handoff and reverse back action. These remain the strongest movement. The
financial reading has a different rule: the data plane stays still while light
and local chart material answer the scroll. BRI blue, navy, clear glass, and the
existing potential/customer colors remain the palette; Geist remains the type.

## The score

| Reading beat | Scroll changes | Reading anchor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KC map to chapter | The existing map/copy handoff enters the selected financial reading. | KC name, chapter and source summary remain explicit. |
| Marketshare | Glass capacity resolves locally, the source fraction fills, and a narrow material glint crosses the glass. | Ratio, legend, period and exact amounts never move with the material. |
| Product change | Lines draw from the prior period toward the current one. | Both sets of labels and axes stay fixed. |
| Mapping opportunity | Sphere material blooms around its recorded center; scroll light crosses the material. | Axis grid, priority region, point center, hit target and unit readout agree throughout. |
| Sector skyline | Local top/side faces settle and customer fill reaches its source fraction. | Potential heights, ratios and sector labels retain their reading coordinates. |
| Ledger and exact table | A short heading light marks progress; heatmap material receives a fine top edge. | Every row, column, cell and sticky header remains flat and aligned. |
| Kinerja composition and trend | Donut walls resolve inside their stage; a timeseries path draws to its actual endpoint. | Numeric source labels, legend and exact-data table remain available. |

No pinned travel is added to the reading. There are no timed loops, hover
rotation, scattered rows, translated bubble centers, or delayed tails that keep
running after input stops. Ordinary data text is not faded or transformed.
Mobile reduces local material depth rather than shrinking a desktop pin track.

## Runtime and CSS contract

The deck adds `.sg-reading-motion` and retains `.sg-scroll-luxury` for existing
components. Existing `data-sg-motion` component kinds remain; `section` marks a
financial block and `coverage` marks its compatible slab when present.

`--reading-enter` is the local 0..1 material reveal. It reaches 1 before the
chart's reading band, then latches until that DOM is replaced. Reversing scroll
does not empty a chart or alter a previously read fraction. `--reading-pass` is
0..1 across visible travel and affects decorative light only. The runtime also
writes `data-reading-phase="before|enter|hold|leave|after"` for inspection and
`data-reading-settled="true"` when the source geometry is fully established.
Reduced motion supplies complete values without material movement.

`reading-motion.css` is imported last. It neutralizes the earlier whole-surface,
row/cell, axis, list and numeric-label reveal transforms. Animated material uses
existing internal elements: vessel body/liquid/glass, prism solid/top/side/fill,
orb span, donut stage/disc/walls, and source line paths. No wrapper is required.
Clipping is restricted to orb material and the donut stage; the glass sheen
travels inside its existing body bounds. Rims, source labels, focus targets,
tables and disclosures are not blanket-clipped.

## Evidence to collect

Inspect identical reading positions after downward and upward scroll; labels,
row/cell rectangles, sphere centers, source ratios and table layout must match.
Capture entry, reading hold and settled states for representative data objects,
and verify the full actual-data content, long disclosures, mobile, reduced
motion, SVG fallback and lateral handoff interruption. Compare the built assets
with the inspected candidate before reporting application completion. This score
records intended behavior; it is not a claim that browser checks have passed.
