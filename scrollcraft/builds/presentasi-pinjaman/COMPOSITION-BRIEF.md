# Composition and presenter control

27 October 2026. Authored implementation decisions under the user's request to reformat and improve the existing presentation.

User direction: use empty space effectively; show Marketshare savings left and loans right simultaneously; make it easy to choose where to present; preserve luxurious scroll transitions. Existing BRI identity, live polygons, source figures and accessibility remain the assets and constraints.

Grammar: a financial comparison workspace with explicit map/detail rooms. Peak: selecting a named KC opens both halves of its market position in one composed frame. No marketing video or filler pin tracks. Scroll World camera continuity is applied to the existing live geometry, as specified by asix-presentation. Scroll Craft supplies layered chart materials, distinct reading stages and mobile composition. The video preflight lacks ffmpeg and playwright-core; this live-chart implementation needs neither video encoding nor generated footage, and browser verification uses the provided Playwright tool.

Palette: BRI blue #0058d0, ink #111820, navy #071d3b, warm credit #a68154, paper #f4f6fa, light blue #bddbff. Retain the installed typography.

Journey and feeling: orientation through a persistent chapter rail; confidence through named KC/Area and paired totals; clarity through eight product vessels and source-derived comparisons; understanding through compact trends/tables; control through adjacent-topic buttons and direct section links. Camera zoom and identity overlay remain the entrance, material assembly the chart reveal, line drawing the time narrative, and exact tables the calm reading plane.

Desktop composition: compact context header; savings and loans share one comparison width. Subsequent evidence uses paired panels. At narrow widths the two comparisons stack at full readable width. Space separates topics, not repeated giant headings or duplicated totals. The presenter can jump anywhere, retain the chosen KC between topics, or return to the full map.

No figure is invented to fill space. Insights describe source ranks/changes, not recommendations. Amounts are never animated through fake intermediate numbers. Short transitions are cancellable; reduced motion remains instant. Validate source fidelity, navigation races, keyboard, mobile, landscape and actual final assets.
